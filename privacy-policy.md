# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what the Twelve Hues app ("the app", "we") does with your information. Twelve Hues is a personal color analysis app published by Arda Sen. Questions: support@ardasenn.com.

## Short version

- You can try color analysis as a guest. For an account you can use on other devices and paid features, sign in with an email code or Google. We do not ask for your name; Google shares it if you choose Google sign-in.
- Photos are sent for analysis, realistic look images, or an optional chat photo after your consent. We do not store them on our server. Device copies and provider retention are explained below.
- We keep your result (measured colors and season) so you can return to it. You can delete it, and your account, at any time in Settings > Delete my account.
- We do not sell your data.

Selfies taken in the app are not saved to your photo gallery. Photos you share yourself (for example a color card) are shared through your phone's share menu and are then handled by the app you choose. You can also pick a photo you already have instead of taking one: we open your phone's picker, read only the photo you choose and never browse your library. The photos you take or pick stay inside the app on this phone, so you do not have to add them again next time. They also stay when you sign out, together with your realistic look images, clothes photos and stylist chat. They are removed when you delete your account, or when a different account signs in on this phone. The app never saves them to your gallery; you can save or share one yourself from the share menu.

The instant hair and makeup try-on runs entirely on your phone. So does reading your skin color from your front selfie: only the resulting numbers (no photo) are added to your analysis and kept with your result. For both, the first time, the app downloads Google's free MediaPipe code and models from jsDelivr and Google. Your photos are never sent to them; like any download, they see your IP address.

## Who processes your data for us

- Anthropic (AI analysis and suggestions). Receives photos and quiz answers for analysis; measured colors and palette for look suggestions; recent messages, profile and wardrobe data, and an optional consented photo for chat. Wardrobe photos are not sent automatically. API data is not used for training by default. Standard API retention is up to 30 days, with contractual, legal and safety exceptions.
- fal.ai (realistic look images, Plus and Max only). Receives your front selfie and the colors you chose (color codes and strength only, no names or text) only to return the image. We send each request with fal.ai's setting not to store it. fal.ai runs OpenAI's image model, so OpenAI also processes the photo.
- OpenAI (image model used by fal.ai). API data is not used for training by default; standard abuse-monitoring retention is up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions.
- Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions). Stores your email address, plan, results, wardrobe, photo and IP fingerprints, realistic image fingerprints, stylist message dates and the AI reports you send, and runs the server code that passes photos to Anthropic and fal.ai.
- Resend (email delivery). Receives your email address to deliver your sign-in code.
- RevenueCat (purchases). Receives your account ID (a random code, not your email address) and your purchase details from Google Play or the App Store, to confirm your purchase and tell our server which plan you own.
- Google Play (app check, Android). When integrity checks are enabled, Google Play checks the app and device before AI requests. It processes app and device information under its own terms; we receive the verdict. This check is not yet enabled in the current version.
- Google (only if you choose Continue with Google). Confirms your Google account and shares your email address, name and profile picture link with us. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries. They act on our instructions and are bound by their data processing terms.

We will update this policy before adding any new provider (for example for ads), and the in-app terms will name it before any photo is sent.

## Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat tells our server which plan you bought. You can ask for a refund within 14 days through the store or by writing to us; a refunded purchase is removed from your account.

## Your choices and rights

- Delete: Settings > Delete my account removes your account, email address and any Google name and picture link, your results, wardrobe, fingerprints and message dates from our server, and the selfies, realistic look images, clothes photos and stylist chat from your phone. We keep only the dates of past analyses, images and messages, with nothing that links them to you, so our daily limits keep working.
- Access, correction, objection: email support@ardasenn.com. We answer within 48 hours and act within 30 days.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, Türkiye under KVKK, or California), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Photo analysis is for people aged 13 or older, or younger users with permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child used the app without permission, contact us and we will delete the data.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each account read its own results, and secret keys are kept on the server, never in the app.

## Accuracy

Color analysis is an estimate, not a medical or professional assessment.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
