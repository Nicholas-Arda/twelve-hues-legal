# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 16, 2026

This policy explains what the Twelve Hues app ("the app", "we") does with your information. Twelve Hues is a personal color analysis app published by Arda Sen. Questions: **support@ardasenn.com**.

## Short version

- You sign in with your email address and a code. We do not ask for your name, and there is no password.
- Your selfies are sent to our server only to be measured. They are **not stored**. They are discarded as soon as the measurement comes back.
- We keep your result (measured colors and season) so you can return to it. You can delete it, and your account, at any time in **Settings > Delete my account**.
- We do not sell your data.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Selfies you take in the app (3 photos) | To measure the natural color of your skin, undertone, eyes and hair, and to check photo quality | Not stored. Resized on your phone, sent over an encrypted connection, passed to our AI provider, discarded when the measurement returns |
| Quiz answers (for example natural hair color) | To help the measurement | Sent with the photos, not stored separately |
| Your result: measured colors, season, confidence, photo quality notes | To show your report and let you come back to it | Until you delete it |
| One-way fingerprints (SHA-256) of each photo | So the same photo always gets the same result and quality check | Until you delete your data |
| Your email address and account ID | To sign you in with a 6–8 digit code and to connect your result to your account on any phone | Until you delete your account |
| Technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and request time) | Security, abuse prevention, fixing errors | According to the provider's standard log retention |

Selfies taken in the app are not saved to your photo gallery. Photos you share yourself (for example a color card) are shared through your phone's share menu and are then handled by the app you choose.

## Who processes your data for us

- **Anthropic** (AI measurement). Receives your selfies and quiz answers only to return a color measurement and a photo quality check. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
- **Supabase** (database, sign-in and server functions). Stores your email address, results and photo fingerprints, and runs the server code that passes photos to Anthropic.
- **Resend** (email delivery). Receives your email address to deliver your sign-in code.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries. They act on our instructions and are bound by their data processing terms.

We will update this policy before adding any new provider (for example for payments or ads), and the in-app terms will name it before any photo is sent.

## Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your card details. Refunds are handled by the store.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete:** Settings > Delete my account removes your account and email address, your results and photo fingerprints from our server, and the selfies from your phone.
- **Access, correction, objection:** email support@ardasenn.com. We answer within 48 hours and act within 30 days.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, Türkiye under KVKK, or California), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Photo analysis is for people aged 13 or older, or younger users with permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child used the app without permission, contact us and we will delete the data.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each account read its own results, and secret keys are kept on the server, never in the app.

## Accuracy

Color analysis is an estimate, not a medical or professional assessment.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
