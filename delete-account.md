# Delete your Twelve Hues account

Twelve Hues is a personal color analysis app published by Arda Sen. You can delete your account and data at any time, with or without the app.

## In the app (immediate)

1. Open Twelve Hues in your current guest session or signed-in account, and tap the settings button (sliders icon) at the top right of the screen.
2. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted from our server right away, and the app removes the photos, images and chat it kept on your phone.

## Without the app

Email **support@ardasenn.com** from the email address you sign in with (for a Google sign-in, your Google email address), with the subject **Delete my account**. We may reply to that address to confirm the request comes from you. We answer within 48 hours and delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

A guest account has no email address. Use the in-app deletion option while you still have access to that guest session; an email address alone cannot identify a guest account.

## What is deleted

- Your account: email address and account ID, and, if you used Google, the name and profile picture link Google shared with us
- Your results: measured and original model colors, chosen eye and hair colors, skin color numbers, season, photo quality notes and the source of the undertone decision
- Your hair and makeup suggestions, your wardrobe (piece names, color codes, scores) and outfits
- Photo and IP fingerprints, realistic image fingerprints, stylist message dates and the AI reports you sent
- On your phone (in-app deletion only): the selfies, realistic look images, clothes photos, stylist chat, recommendations and attached chat photos the app kept. If you delete by email, uninstalling the app removes them.

Your selfies and attached chat photos were never stored on our server.

## What is kept

- Only anonymous dates of past analysis, image and chat requests remain for our global usage limits. Account IDs, IP fingerprints and image request fingerprints are removed from these records.
- Your purchase record stays with Google Play or the App Store, under their rules.
- Our hosting provider keeps technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for its standard retention period.
- AI providers apply their own retention rules and legal or safety exceptions, described in our Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not promise immediate deletion from every provider.
